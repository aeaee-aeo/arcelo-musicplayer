About "BE"

BE is BTS' fifth Korean-language album, following historical smash hit single “Dynamite.” The latter speaks on behalf of everyone stuck in the new normality brought on by COVID-19: one day, the world stopped without any warning, streets erased of footsteps and the way out is nowhere near in sight.
