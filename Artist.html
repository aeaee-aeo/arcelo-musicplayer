Artists

Bruno Mars

An American singer, songwriter, and record producer known for his smooth pop, R&B, and funk-infused music. He is celebrated for his energetic performances, catchy songs like "Uptown Funk" and "Just the Way You Are".

Avril Lavigne

A Canadian singer-songwriter and musician, Avril became famous in the early 2000s with her punk-rock-inspired pop hits like "Complicated" and "Sk8er Boi." She is known for her rebellious attitude and empowering lyrics.

BTS

A globally popular South Korean boy band known for their intricate choreography, powerful performances, and heartfelt lyrics. BTS blends various genres including K-pop, hip-hop, and pop, and has a massive fanbase worldwide, known as ARMY.

SEVENTEEN

A South Korean boy band consisting of 13 members, SEVENTEEN is known for their self-producing abilities, with members involved in songwriting, choreography, and production. Their music varies from pop to hip-hop to EDM.

Bellie Eilish

An American singer and songwriter known for her unique blend of alternative pop and electropop, often characterized by her hushed vocals. Hits like "bad guy" and "everything i wanted" have made her a global sensation.

Lady Gaga

An American singer, songwriter, and actress, Lady Gaga is known for her eclectic style, powerful vocals, and boundary-pushing music. With hits like "Poker Face" and "Bad Romance," she has made a major impact in pop culture.

LAYYI

A relatively new and emerging artist with a distinctive sound blending modern pop, R&B, and electronic music. LAYYI is known for her unique musical style and strong voice, slowly gaining recognition in the music scene.

Rosé

A South Korean singer and member of the global K-pop group BLACKPINK, Rosé is known for her powerful and emotive vocals. She has also pursued a successful solo career with hits like "On The Ground" and "GONE."
